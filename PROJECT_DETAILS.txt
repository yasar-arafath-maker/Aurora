1. PROJECT MISSION & PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Traditional rooftop solar sales and engineering assessments rely on static 2D
aerial satellite imagery, slow CAD modeling software, or inaccurate estimation
calculators. This causes high customer drop-off, inaccurate shading calculations,
misaligned expectations, and costly site redesign visits.

SolarFit AR v2 bridges this gap by providing an instant, browser-based, zero-install
augmented reality (AR) and 3D simulation platform. Users can point their phone
camera at their rooftop or open a 3D orbit simulation on their desktop to test
physical 1:1 scale solar panels, analyze real sunlight trajectories, simulate
thermal heat derating, test obstacle shading, and receive instant AI recommendations.


2. PHOTOVOLTAIC (PV) TECHNOLOGY CATALOGUE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The application integrates six distinct industry solar cell technologies, each
modeled with accurate PBR optical properties, efficiency, and temperature coefficients:

1. MONOCRYSTALLINE (P-Type Standard)
   - Efficiency: ~21.0%
   - Temp Coefficient: -0.36% / °C
   - Aesthetics: Sleek dark obsidian crystal, anti-reflective micro-texture.
   - Ideal For: Standard residential rooftops with moderate area constraints.

2. POLYCRYSTALLINE (Legacy Multi-Crystal)
   - Efficiency: ~16.0%
   - Temp Coefficient: -0.42% / °C
   - Aesthetics: Deep blue flake shimmer, visible wafer boundary grains.
   - Status: Legacy / Phased-out. Highlights to users why modern upgrades yield 40% more.

3. THIN-FILM (Cadmium Telluride / CIGS)
   - Efficiency: ~11.5%
   - Temp Coefficient: -0.21% / °C (High thermal resilience)
   - Aesthetics: Uniform matte dark charcoal, lightweight frameless glass.
   - Ideal For: High-temperature desert environments or curved architectural roofs.

4. ADVANCED MONO PERC (Passivated Emitter & Rear Contact)
   - Efficiency: ~22.5%
   - Temp Coefficient: -0.34% / °C
   - Aesthetics: Deep sapphire-black tint, dielectric passivation layer.
   - Ideal For: Cost-to-efficiency balance in residential and commercial rooftops.

5. ADVANCED TOPCon (Tunnel Oxide Passivated Contact — N-Type)
   - Efficiency: ~24.0%
   - Temp Coefficient: -0.30% / °C
   - Degradation: Ultra-low LID (Light Induced Degradation) < 1.0% in year 1.
   - Aesthetics: Seamless velvet black, 16-busbar micro-grid lines.
   - Status: Top AI Recommendation for hot urban rooftops with high space constraints.

6. BIFACIAL DUAL-GLASS (Rear-Contact Albedo Harvesting)
   - Efficiency: ~22.8% front + up to 30% rear harvest
   - Bifaciality Factor: 80% (0.80)
   - Architecture: Dual-tempered glass, elevated mounting clearance (+0.45m).
   - Ideal For: High-albedo surfaces (White TPO membrane roofs, crushed stone, snow).


3. ENVIRONMENTAL & ROOFTOP SURFACE MATERIAL MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SolarFit AR simulates the physics of roof surface reflections (albedo coefficient):

- Standard Grey Concrete: Albedo = 0.20 (Standard diffuse reflection)
- White TPO / PVC Membrane: Albedo = 0.75 (High reflectivity, ideal for Bifacial)
- Crushed Gravel / Stone: Albedo = 0.18 (Moderate diffuse scattering)
- Metal Zinc / Standing Seam: Albedo = 0.15 (Direct specular glare reflection)


4. HARDWARE & SENSOR INTEGRATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Camera Stream: WebRTC `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia` with video frame sampling.
- Spatial Tracking: WebXR `hit-test` with local-floor reference spaces.
- Orientation Tracking: Web DeviceOrientationEvent (Alpha, Beta, Gamma) with compass heading.
- Sun Tracking: Astronomical solar positioning engine calculating Solar Zenith,
  Azimuth, Hour Angle, and Declination for user's geographical coordinates.
- Ambient Light Probe: Real-time scene brightness estimation from camera video luma.
