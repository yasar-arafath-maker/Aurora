1. INTRODUCTION & CORE CONCEPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SolarFit AR v2 is a next-generation augmented reality (WebXR) and 3D web-based
solar energy rooftop simulation platform. It enables solar engineers, residential
homeowners, and commercial facility managers to visualize, configure, and
analyze 1:1 physical-scale photovoltaic (PV) solar installations directly on their
roof or ground in real-time.

The application operates in two seamless modes:
1. WebXR Augmented Reality Mode (on supported mobile devices with camera pass-through)
2. Interactive 3D Orbit / Fallback Mode (on desktop browsers and non-XR devices)


2. END-TO-END STEP-BY-STEP USER WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STAGE 1: HARDWARE PROBING & PERMISSION OVERLAY
- When the application starts, it performs immediate diagnostic checks:
  * Camera device check (MediaDevices API)
  * WebXR immersive-ar session support check
  * Device Orientation & Compass Sensors availability
  * Location & Solar trajectory calculations
- An initial Glassmorphism HUD displays two options:
  [Start AR Session] -> Requests WebXR and hardware camera pass-through.
  [Continue in Demo Mode (No camera)] -> Launches high-fidelity 3D Orbit mode.

STAGE 2: SURFACE SCANNING & BOUNDARY DETECTION
- The application enters the scanning state (`enterScanning()`):
  * A procedural holographic grid expands across the virtual floor/roof plane.
  * 420 animated floating feature tracking points indicate active spatial mesh.
  * A central reticle follows the user's camera raycast.
  * Once the tracking ray maintains stability for >700ms (`state.validT > 0.7s`),
    the spatial surface locks automatically.
  * The toast status updates to: "Surface locked — tap button to place."
  * The primary Floating Action Button (#fab) becomes enabled with an emerald glow.

STAGE 3: ANTIGRAVITY ASSEMBLY & TOUCHDOWN (1:1 SCALE PLACEMENT)
- When the user taps "#fab", the placement sequence triggers:
  * The virtual array anchor is locked to the 3D surface coordinates.
  * Sound Synthesizer plays a kinetic whoosh sound (`playWhoosh()`).
  * Antigravity Phase: Individual solar cells fly outward in a 3D vortex swarm.
  * Procedural electric arc beams bridge between adjacent panel contact rails.
  * Holographic techno rings rotate dynamically around the array footprint.
  * The array gently descends and lands on the rooftop with a realistic
    physical squash-and-settle spring animation (`playImpact()`).
  * The perimeter bounding box computes real-time roof fitment ("✓ Perfect fit").

STAGE 4: INTERACTIVE 3D SCI-FI SYSTEMS (QUICK FX TOOLBAR)
Once placed, the Advanced FX toolbar appears with 5 cutting-edge toggles:

1. [🔥 IR Thermal Cam] (#thermalModeBtn)
   - Toggles custom GLSL FLIR Ironbow thermal heatmap shader material.
   - Calculates real-time cell temperatures based on ambient heat and irradiance.
   - Visually renders thermal hot-spots, bypass diode gradients, and heat derating.
   - Button label toggles between "IR Thermal Cam" and "Normal Cam".

2. [💨 Wind Aerodynamics] (#windModeBtn)
   - Toggles dynamic 3D aerodynamic airflow streamline particles over the panels.
   - Visualizes air cooling velocity, boundary layer friction, and uplift forces.
   - Plays aerodynamic ambient sound synthesis (`playWind()`).
   - Button label toggles between "Wind Aerodynamics" and "Hide Wind".

3. [📊 3D Module HUDs] (#hologramHudBtn)
   - Projects floating 3D holographic telemetry billboard cards above each module.
   - Displays real-time per-panel metrics: Instantaneous Watts (e.g. 400W),
     Operating Voltage (Vmp), Current (Imp), and Cell Temp (°C).
   - Button label toggles between "3D Module HUDs" and "Hide HUDs".

4. [🌲 Shade Obstacle] (#obstacleBtn)
   - Places a 3D rooftop obstruction unit (chimney/HVAC/tree) beside the array.
   - Casts real-time directional PBR shadows directly across solar panel cells.
   - Real-time derating engine recalculates string clipping and mismatch losses.
   - Button label toggles between "Shade Obstacle" and "Remove Obstacle".

5. [🎬 Cine Flyover] (#cineTourBtn)
   - Triggers an automated cinematic 360-degree orbital drone flyover camera.
   - Plays chime soundscape (`playCineChime()`) and showcases the installation.

STAGE 5: AI SOLAR DECISION ADVISOR (MCDA + XAI)
- Activated via the top header badge or button (#aiAdvisorBtn):
  * Evaluates rooftop material albedo (Concrete 0.20, White TPO 0.75, Gravel, Metal).
  * Runs multi-criteria decision matrix analyzing:
    Efficiency (30%), Thermal resilience (22%), Bifacial albedo gain (20%),
    and 25-year reliability (28%).
  * Recommends the optimal technology (e.g., Advanced TOPCon at 86-98% match).
  * Provides explainable AI bullet points detailing why the technology was chosen.
  * "Apply AI Recommendation" automatically switches the 3D model and aligns
    azimuth orientation to True South for maximum solar harvesting.

STAGE 6: 3D DYNAMIC ARRAY BUILDER & TILT CUSTOMIZER
- Activated via the [📐 Layout] button (#layoutBtn):
  * Columns Slider (1 to 6 modules wide): live procedural rebuild of racking.
  * Rows Slider (1 to 4 rows deep): adjusts total module count in real time.
  * Tilt Angle Slider (0° to 45°): dynamically tilts panels on ballasted mounts.
  * Inter-Row Pitch Spacing Slider (1.2m to 3.0m): expands spacing to eliminate
    winter solstice inter-row shading.
  * Real-time metrics recalculate instantly: Total Panels, Total Capacity (kWp),
    Total Footprint Area (m²), and Perimeter Fitment Status ("✓ Safe Fit").

STAGE 7: 25-YEAR SOLAR ROI & BILL SAVINGS SIMULATOR
- Activated via the [💰 ROI] button (#roiBtn):
  * Interactive Monthly Power Bill slider ($40 to $600/month).
  * Computes 25-Year Net Utility Savings with 3.5% annual utility rate escalation.
  * Computes Payback Period (years to breakeven after federal incentives).
  * Environmental Impact Calculator: Metric tons of CO2 offset, mature trees
    planted equivalent, and gasoline vehicle offset equivalents.

STAGE 8: 3D COMPONENT RAYCAST INSPECTOR & EXPLODED VIEW
- Users can click/tap any part of the 3D solar array:
  * Three.js Raycaster identifies the exact sub-component clicked (Front Glass,
    Silicon Wafer, Busbars, EVA encapsulant, Junction Box, Inverter, Aluminum Rail).
  * Pops up a Glassmorphism technical inspector card detailing specs and warranties.
  * "Toggle 3D Exploded View" smoothly separates the components into an exploded
    kinetic stack for engineering education.

STAGE 9: AR SNAPSHOT & CLIENT PROPOSAL EXPORT
- Activated via the [📸 Snapshot] button (#snapshotBtn):
  * Captures full-resolution canvas with 3D overlay, statistics, and timestamp.
  * Generates an instant preview modal with client-ready proposal framing.
  * One-click download as PNG ("solarfit-rooftop-proposal.png").
